# Gyre Energy

> Gyre Energy is a UK-based climate-tech company that reduces cooling costs for cold storage facilities, data centers, and building portfolios by combining artificial intelligence with thermal energy storage. Gyre AI, a digital twin, shifts cooling cycles to run when grid energy is cheapest and greenest, paired with an integrated thermal energy storage "cold battery" and a real-time monitoring and controls layer. The company is a Speedinvest portfolio company and currently exposes no public developer API.
